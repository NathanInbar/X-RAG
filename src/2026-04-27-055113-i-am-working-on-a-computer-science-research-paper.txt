  MINEA Methodology Summary

  Core Concept:
  - Generate synthetic "needles" (artificial entities with properties) that are
  contextually relevant but don't exist in the original document
  - Inject these needles as natural text into the document
  - Run information extraction
  - Measure what % of needles were successfully extracted
  - This creates synthetic ground truth without manual annotation

  Adapting MINEA for Triple Extraction Evaluation

  For your GraphRAG research, we can design an experiment that:

  1. Generates "Needle Triples"
  - Use an LLM to create factual statements (subject-predicate-object triples)
  that:
    - Are thematically consistent with your source corpus
    - Don't exist in the original text
    - Have clear, identifiable entities

  2. Injects Needles as Natural Text
  - Convert needle triples into natural language sentences
  - Insert them into source text chunks (10-30% of total content per the paper)
  - Maintain document coherence

  3. Runs Your Existing Triple Extraction
  - Process needle-infused documents through your _ExtractTriples DSPy signature
  - Same preprocessing pipeline you already use

  4. Measures Extraction Success
  - Multiple identification criteria (like in Table 5 of the paper):
    - Exact match: Does extracted triple exactly match needle?
    - Semantic match: Embedding similarity between needle and extracted triples
    - Keyword overlap: Are S/P/O terms from needle present in extracted triples?
    - LLM-as-judge: Ask LLM if needle was captured in extraction

  5. Computes MINEA Score
  - Overall: # successfully extracted needles / # total needles
  - Per-type analysis (if using different relation types)
  - Per-method comparison (LeanRAG vs KG-Gen vs HypergraphRAG)

  Key Advantages for Your Research

  1. Objective ground truth - no manual annotation needed
  2. Method comparison - directly compare your 3 GraphRAG implementations
  3. Model comparison - extend your existing triplet_model_comparison.py with
  MINEA scores
  4. Complements existing metrics - MINEA measures recall (what's captured),
  your existing judge metrics measure precision/faithfulness

  A. Needle Generation Scope:
  - Should needles cover all relation types equally, or focus on your domain
  (what's in your corpus)?
  - Do you want to control for triple complexity (simple facts vs. complex

  B. Integration with Existing Code:

  - Per-method comparison (main goal)?
